=====================================================================================================================
0. QUESTION
=====================================================================================================================
Does a different, stronger DECODER for the same latent space give a sharper, more faithful right eye from the latents
the UNet already produces (origin and the shipped deliverable), with no retraining of the generator, and without
invented texture, splatting stripes or flicker?

STEP 1 (done, before this file): SVD's VAE encoder and quant_conv are bit-identical to the sd-vae-ft-mse / sd-vae-ft-ema
encoders (fp32 files: 100/100 name-matched tensors max|d| 0, plus the 8 mid-block attention tensors after the legacy name
mapping, COMPAT_ATTN_v1.json) and to the SD1.5 base VAE / consistency-decoder encoders at fp16 (108/108).  fp32 encodes of
14 real-right-eye frames of 0040 and 0268 by the SVD VAE and by sd-vae-ft-mse are bit-identical (max|d| 0).  So the UNet's
latents ARE SD kl-f8 latents; any kl-f8 decoder can read them.  Scaling: every candidate receives the UNSCALED latent
z = latent / 0.18215 (AutoencoderKL.decode takes z; ConsistencyDecoderVAE.decode takes z and itself computes
(z * 0.18215 - means) / stds); all configs carry scaling_factor 0.18215.

=====================================================================================================================
1. DECODERS
=====================================================================================================================
Input to every decoder: the captured bf16 latent window L ([1,14,4,72,128], scaled), z_bf16 = bf16(1/0.18215 * L)
computed exactly as the deployed decode_latents does, then (for non-bf16 decoders) upcast/cast EXACTLY to the decoder's
dtype (bf16 -> fp32 and bf16 -> fp16 are exact for these values), so every decoder sees the same input values.
Output path for every decoder: [-1,1] frames -> the pipeline's tensor2vid(output_type="pil") -> np.array -> /255 ->
x255 -> uint8 (redecode_v1.py's path, verbatim), keep rule of inpainting_inference.main (14/3 windows).
  stock    BASELINE.  SVD temporal decoder, bf16, decode_latents(decode_chunk_size=2) on the full window (deployed path).
           Gate: SBS md5 == the capture render md5 (decoder_ft capture, C1-gated against the published renders).
  stock32  CONTROL (never selectable, never tested).  The same temporal decoder in fp32 (cudnn TF32 default), same full
           window, chunk 2.  Purpose: separate the precision effect (stock32 - stock) from the decoder effect
           (candidate - stock32).
  ftmse    CANDIDATE.  stabilityai/sd-vae-ft-mse AutoencoderKL (post_quant_conv + decoder), fp32, per frame (batch 2).
  ftema    CANDIDATE.  stabilityai/sd-vae-ft-ema, same as ftmse.
  cd       CANDIDATE.  openai/consistency-decoder (ConsistencyDecoderVAE, fp16 weights as released, 2 steps), per frame,
           batch 1, a fresh torch.Generator('cuda').manual_seed(20261005) before EVERY frame (identical noise for every
           frame: frame-constant noise, chosen to avoid seed flicker); assert finite output.  Not via decode_latents.
  sd15     HEADROOM-ONLY reference: the original SD1.5 kl-f8 decoder (VideoPainter sd15_base copy, fp16 file, run fp32).
Per-frame decoders decode only the frames the keep rule retains (exact: no state crosses frames).  Timing: GPU-
synchronised decode seconds per clip and per decoded frame are recorded; for per-frame decoders the deployed-structure
cost (per-frame time x 14 x n_windows, since the pipeline decodes whole windows) is also reported.

3. STEP 4 -- DEV EVALUATION ON THE MODELS' OWN LATENTS (the selection)
=====================================================================================================================
Cells: 6 dev clips x {origin_cap, deliv_cap} (decoder_ft captures).  Rows: stock, stock32, ftmse, ftema, cd, each an SBS
FFV1 whose left half is the capture render's left half (identical for every row of a clip).
Gates before any score is used:  D1 stock SBS md5 == capture md5 on all 12 cells.  D2 the registered/UNREG scorer stock
rows reproduce decoder_ft (REG_FRAME 5-clip means origin 0.2568 / deliverable 0.2507, UNREG 6-clip 0.4616 / 0.4561,
TABLE_DEV_main_v1.txt) within 1e-4.  D3 cd decoded twice on one window gives identical uint8 output.
Scorers (byte-identical copies in this dir, md5 in TOOL_MD5_v1.txt): score_clip_ll.py (UNREG, SCORE_STEP 4, stock rows),
make_rows (shared left half, decoder_ft make_rows_shared_v1.py logic), score_registered_df_v1.py (REG_FRAME / REG_CLIP /
BLK_LOCAL LPIPS-Alex, rPSNR), score_aux_v1.py (DISTS, NIQE, MUSIQ, LPIPS-VGG, decompose on the REG_FRAME-registered GT),
score_temporal_ll.py (tLP, RAFT warp error with flow on the GT window, window-seam ratio; all frames), score_pairflicker_v1.py.
Registered metrics: 5 clips (0040 0082 0184 0245 0268) -- 0091's real right eye lies outside the scorer's [-120,40] px grid,
as in decoder_ft/scale_gt; this is why the counts below are "of 5", not "of 6".  UNREG, NIQE, MUSIQ, temporal: 6 clips.
Every delta = candidate minus stock on the SAME latents (same clip, same model).

PASS RULE for a candidate D (all must hold):
  P1  deliverable: REG_FRAME LPIPS-Alex better than stock on >= 4 of 5 registered dev clips AND 5-clip mean change < 0
  P2  origin:      REG_FRAME 5-clip mean change <= 0            (the gain must not be specific to one model's latents)
  G1  deliverable: NIQE 6-clip mean change <= +0.05              (blur_diag seed yardstick 0.052; lower NIQE = better)
  G2  deliverable: registered PSNR (REG_FRAME rPSNR) 5-clip mean change >= -0.30 dB
  G3  deliverable: UNREG LPIPS-Alex 6-clip mean change <= +0.001
  G4  deliverable: RAFT warp error: 6-clip mean of per-clip ratio D/stock <= 1.10, OR D's 6-clip mean warp <= the real
      right eye's 6-clip mean warp (a genuinely sharper frame raises warp error without flicker; GT is the ceiling)
  G5  deliverable: window-seam ratio 6-clip mean change <= +0.05
  G6  deliverable: no invented texture / stripes: 5-clip mean of per-clip ratio D/stock <= 1.10 for stripeE AND for flatHF,
      unless D's 5-clip mean value stays <= the registered real right eye's (score_aux GT row)
SELECTION: if several pass, the lowest deliverable 5-clip mean REG_FRAME change; within 0.0005 of it -> the cheaper decoder
(ftmse/ftema before cd).  EXACTLY ONE candidate (or none) goes to test.  stock32 is reported, never selected.
Reported, not gating: REG_CLIP, BLK_LOCAL, DISTS, MUSIQ, LPIPS-VGG, tLP, edgeHF, haloFrac, decode-pair flicker ratio
(meaningless for per-frame decoders, which have no pairs), per-clip values, origin's guards, and the GT-free unsharp
reference from decoder_ft (us_s1a0.15 dREG +0.0002, TABLE_DEV_main_v1.txt; same stock rows, md5-identical).
Diagnostic DS-SEED (never selection): cd with seeds 1 and 2 on the kept frames of windows 0, 4, 8 of 0040 and 0268
(deliverable latents): mean |cd_s - cd_s'| in render-geometry regions defined from the model input BR (edge = top decile of
BR gradient, flat = bottom half, both excluding the dilated disocclusion mask) and in the dilated hole, next to |cd - stock|
and |ftmse - stock| in the same regions.  Seed-dependent energy is, by construction, not information from the latent.
