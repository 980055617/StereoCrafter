0. ANSWER
=====================================================================================================================================
NO -- not under the pre-registered rule.  None of the three swap-in decoders (sd-vae-ft-mse, sd-vae-ft-ema, OpenAI consistency decoder)
passed on dev, so nothing went to the 12 test clips (PREREG section 3; SELECTION_DEV_v1.json "selected": null).
  * They CAN read the UNet's latents (step 1: SVD's encoder IS the SD kl-f8 encoder, bit-identical), and on CLEAN latents of the real
    right eye they are much better decoders than SVD's: decoder-only round trip LPIPS-Alex 0.0452 (stock) -> 0.0350 (ft-mse, 6/6
    clips better, PSNR +0.23 dB) / 0.0344 (ft-ema) / 0.0412 (consistency) [TABLE_HEADROOM_v1.txt].
  * On the UNet's OWN latents the gain shrinks to a small one and comes with added texture.  Best case ft-mse on the deliverable:
    registered LPIPS (REG_FRAME) -0.0026 (5/5 dev clips better), UNREG -0.0018, NIQE -0.90 (6/6), registered PSNR -0.04 dB, warp
    error x1.07 -- but flat-region high-frequency energy x1.27 and stripe energy x1.17 vs the stock decoder (guard G6, limit 1.10;
    stock's flat energy is already 1.65x the real eye's, ft-mse's 2.05x) [TABLE_DEV_v1.txt].  -0.0026 is also below the 0.005
    "visible gain" tier.  ft-ema: same picture, stronger (flat x1.34, warp x1.15, only 3/5 clips better).  Consistency decoder: no
    registered gain on the deliverable (-0.00003), flat x1.34, seam ratio +0.06, ~10x the decode time, and its edge detail is set by
    its noise seed, not by the latent (section 4).
  * Visually (section 6): at 100 % ft-mse is only slightly crisper than stock in textured crops and indistinguishable elsewhere -- a
    modest local crispening, not a visibly sharper image, and both stay far softer than the real eye; at 2-4x the candidates add
    crisper edges plus fine grain; no new stripes or visible ringing; the consistency decoder re-draws small structures (embossed
    lettering) differently.
  * Against ORIGIN (dev, 5 registered clips, REG_FRAME; NOT test): origin + stock decoder 0.2568; deliverable + stock 0.2507 (-0.0061);
    deliverable + ft-mse 0.2481 (-0.0087, of which -0.0026 is the decoder's); origin + ft-mse 0.2535 (-0.0033) [TABLE_DEV_v1.txt
    absolute levels].  The published test-set figure for the deliverable vs origin is REG_FRAME -0.0132, 11/12
    [scripts/distill/runs/more_20261004/eval_robustness/TABLE_ROBUSTNESS_v1.txt] -- a different clip set, not comparable.
  * Precision is not the reason: the same SVD decoder in fp32 (control "stock32") is indistinguishable from the deployed bf16 one
    (REG_FRAME -0.0000, flat x0.95).
  * Cost/time: this lane needed no training -- about 2 h of wall time on 2 GPUs for the whole dev evaluation.  A swap is free at run
    time for ft-mse/ft-ema (0.165 s/frame vs 0.176 s/frame stock) and ~10x for the consistency decoder (1.71 s/frame, ~+5 min per
    150-frame clip on top of a ~3 min render).  Replacing the VAE with one that has a DIFFERENT latent space (e.g. 16-channel video
    VAEs) is a different job, not a longer version of this one: the whole generator must be retrained for the new latent space (new
    input/output layers and every layer's statistics).  For scale, the deep round's GT fine-tune moved only 15 attn1 tensors of
    up_blocks.3 for 3000 steps (52 min, 1.03 s/step) and still made outputs worse (test REG_FRAME +0.0466, 12/12 clips)
    [scripts/distill/runs/deep_20261004/scale_gt/RESULTS.txt]; its decoder fine-tune (63.6M decoder parameters, 2000 steps, 5908 s)
    failed its dev gate [deep_20261004/decoder_ft/RESULTS.txt].  No GPU-hour figure for a full retrain is claimed here.
  * Reading (an interpretation, not a test): the decoder headroom is real on clean latents but mostly does not reach the generated
    latents; what a stronger decoder adds there is texture of a plausible kind, which helps no-reference scores (NIQE) much more than
    fidelity to the real eye.  This is consistent with the missing detail not being in the UNet's latents in a form a decoder can
    recover (the registered metric's content/registration floor also dilutes any decoder gain; the two LPIPS numbers above are not
    on the same scale and are not compared as a ratio).

=====================================================================================================================================
8. WHAT THIS CHANGES IN THE EARLIER SUMMARY (deep_20261004: "ぼやけの最大の原因は VAE", "根本対策は VAE の交換")
=====================================================================================================================================
* Swapping the DECODER half of the VAE is not the fix: three stronger decoders, no retraining, small dev gains that come with added
  texture, none passes; the decoder_ft fine-tune also failed.
* The blur claim should be scoped: the VAE round trip does lose detail, and on CLEAN latents part of that loss is decoder capacity
  (ft-mse removes ~23 % of the round-trip LPIPS).  On the UNet's latents a better decoder barely helps, so the blur that matters for
  the outputs sits in the latent / generator path (what the UNet writes into the 4-channel latent), not in the decoder.
* This lane neither supports nor rules out a full VAE replacement with a different, richer latent space: that changes the latent
  itself, which is exactly what a decoder swap cannot do -- and it requires retraining the generator.
