0. THE ANSWER IN FIVE LINES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The published GT is misregistered by 10-60 px against the renders, and that misregistration is about 40 % of the
  published absolute LPIPS.  Registering the real right eye to the model's own input lowers origin from 0.3933 to
  0.2432 (-38 %; -47 % on the 4400-px clips).  So the absolute numbers are NOT robust.
- The DELTAS are robust.  Deliverable vs origin: -0.0128 unregistered, -0.0132 registered per frame, -0.0133
  registered per clip.  The 95 % CI excludes 0 under all six GT variants.  Every pre-registered verdict passes
  (V1-V4).
- "12/12 clips improved" is NOT robust: it is 11/12 under every registered variant.  Clip 0259 flips to +0.0019.
  That is smaller than the change one re-seed produced on 0259 (0.0038, measured on the T5 configuration).
- The gain is spread evenly over frames: UNIFORM by the pre-registered rule.  It does not depend on sampler windows
  and is not concentrated in disocclusion frames.
- The claim rests on n = 12 clips, not 456 frames.  Adding "the 8 dev clips" is NOT clean: only 0082 and 0268
  qualify, and neither has been rendered.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The pre-registered rule gives UNIFORM under both UNREG and REG_FRAME, for the deliverable, T5 and s25.
- Median clip: 100 % of frames improved, top-20 % share of the gain 0.30.  A perfectly uniform gain would give
  0.20; "concentrated" starts at 0.60.
- Pooled over frames: 92 % of 456 frames improve registered, 96 % unregistered.
- 10 clips are uniform.  The exceptions are the two clips whose delta is within the re-seed proxy: 0259 (39 % of
  frames improved, deltas swing +-0.02) and 0042 (71 %, top-20 % share 0.61).
- No sampler-window structure: window 0 averages -0.0133, later windows -0.0132; clip-centred position bins all
  sit within +-0.0011.

  negative).  The gain is frame-wide, consistent with the review finding that the extra sharpness is frame-wide.
- Why the clip is the unit: the within-clip SD of per-frame deltas is 0.0070, the between-clip SD of clip means
  0.0108, and the lag-1 autocorrelation (frames 4 apart) +0.38.

6. WHAT THE THESIS SHOULD REPORT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Keep the published unregistered table as the headline, for comparability with every earlier number, and add
    one registered row plus one sentence:
      "The real right eye is horizontally misregistered against the renders by 10-60 px.  With a GT registered to
       the model input, absolute LPIPS falls by 38 % (origin 0.393 -> 0.243), and the deliverable's improvement is
       unchanged: -0.0128 -> -0.0132, 95 % CI [-0.0196, -0.0079], 11/12 clips."
(b) Replace "12/12 clips" with: "12/12 (11/12 with registered GT; the one exception, 0259 at +0.0019, is smaller
    than the change a single re-seed produced on that clip, 0.0038, measured on the T5 configuration)".  Do not
    present 12/12 as a robust property.
(c) Give CIs and exact tests: -0.0128 [-0.0187, -0.0081], sign test p = 0.00049 (registered: 0.0063); state n = 12
    clips as the unit and that the bootstrap CI is descriptive at this n.
(d) Absolute LPIPS values (0.39 / 0.24) should not be compared with external literature without saying which GT
    geometry was used.
(e) Report T5@1.00 as a SPEED configuration that is non-inferior within the pre-registered margin:
    +0.0011 [+0.0004, +0.0019] vs the deployed deliverable, the same registered.  It still improves on origin,
    but registered that is 10/12 and fragile, and it was selected on these clips.  Do not headline its "12/12".
(f) "Matches the 25-step teacher": the deliverable - s25 difference is +0.0018 to +0.0021, CI spans 0, i.e. about
    87-88 % of the teacher's gain.  "Statistically indistinguishable at n=12" is fair; "equal" is not.
(g) State that the test clips are held out for the weights (both stages clean, base included) but were reused for
    design decisions.  The clean confirmation would be renders on dev 0082 + 0268, reported separately.

7. LIMITATIONS OF THIS CHECK
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- An integer global shift registers ONE depth layer.  0125 (near trunk) and 0259 (near dandelion) are two-layer
  scenes where no global shift is right.  The block-local variant (BLK_LOCAL) agrees with REG_FRAME (-0.0127,
  11/12), so the conclusion does not hinge on this, but absolute registered values carry about 0.002-0.004 of
  shift-choice sensitivity per clip.  For example, on 0042 origin is 0.1564 REG_CLIP vs 0.1599 REG_FRAME.
- LPIPS is still the only quality axis here.  FLICKER (RAFT warp error +22 %) and the stripe amplification from the
  review are untouched by registration.
- One seed per clip.  The seed yardstick is a proxy: it is measured on T5, not on the paired delta, and window 0
  shares its noise.  A real seed study (re-render origin and deliverable with 2-3 seeds on 0042/0259) is outside
  this LPIPS-only lane.
