======================================================================================================================
trainable vae.decoder.* only (63.58M), fp32 masters initialised from the deployed fp16 weights (exact upcast: step 0 cast
to bf16 == the deployed decoder); encoder frozen (it makes the UNet's conditioning).  Input = the deployed scaling round
