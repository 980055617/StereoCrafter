M1 CAMERA DIFFERENCES, left vs real right eye, per clip, per family (Q1)
  Data versions: (T) train tile TL vs TR (what the metric sees), (V) left_eye_v2 vs right_eye_v2 (crf-12 sources,
  the cameras themselves), on the deployed window, test clips at S10, plus a broad sample of VALID TRAIN clips
  (24 per family, every k-th of the sorted valid list, 3 frames 10/40/70; G0 applied to each).
  (a) band amplitude ratio rho_b = sqrt(P_R(b)/P_L(b)), Hann-windowed radially averaged power of Y, bands
      b1 [1/32,1/16) b2 [1/16,1/8) b3 [1/8,1/4) b4 [1/4,1/2] cycles/px (shift-invariant: no registration needed).
  (b) mean-gradient ratio sharp(R)/sharp(L), sharp = score_clip_ll definition (mean |horizontal diff| of RGB).
  (c) relative blur on RAFT-consistent pixels (test clips, S10): sigma_R = argmin_s MSE(G_s*Lw, R),
      sigma_L = argmin_s MSE(Lw, G_s*R), s in {0,0.25,...,2.5}, after a per-frame luma gain/offset fit;
      sigma_rel = sigma_R - sigma_L (> 0: right eye softer).  Control: the same estimator on (R, R round-tripped
      through the forward+backward warp) gives the warp-induced bias, which is subtracted and reported.
  (d) exposure/colour on the registered overlap: mean Y difference (8-bit), contrast ratio std(Y_R)/std(Y_L),
      CIELAB mean offsets (dL*, da*, db*), PSNR gain of a per-clip affine 3x4 colour fit.
  (e) noise sigma (Immerkaer 1996) of Y, 8-bit units, L and R.
  RULE R1: family F "GT SOFTER than input" iff median rho_b3 < 0.87 in BOTH (T) and (V) [test+train clips pooled];
     "GT SHARPER than input" iff median rho_b3 > 1.15 in both; else "no material asymmetry".  Only (T) -> attribute
     to the bundle codec, not the camera.   RULE R2: sigma_rel material iff |median bias-corrected sigma_rel| >= 0.5 px.
