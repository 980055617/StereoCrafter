FIT (fit_scale_v1.py, GPU, model-free): frames 0,8,16,.. < n_GT; disp' = s*disp_deployed + o; estimator = the
registered scorer's PSNR(real right eye TR, warped input) at the deployed window, holes excluded, over integer GT
shifts ddy in [-4,4], ddx in [-240,80] (o = -ddx); common support per stage (holes of ANY candidate excluded for all);
objective = PSNR of the SSE summed over the fit frames at one clip-level shift.  Coarse s = 0.50..5.00 step 0.25,
fine = coarse best +-0.25 step 0.05 plus 1.00.  s* = fine argmax; a boundary hit (0.50 or 5.00) is reported.

DECISION RULES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R1-fidelity (reported, not a gate): R1 vs DEP per (clip, model): |dUNREG|, |dREG_FRAME|.  R1 is REPRESENTATIVE of
  the deployed input iff mean |d| <= 0.003 and max |d| <= 0.006 over the 8 cells (UNREG and REG_FRAME separately).
  Every variant contrast is against R1 regardless; if R1 is not representative this is flagged on every conclusion.

PROBE 1 (geometry).  Contrast RF - R1, per model, 4 clips.
  P1 PRIMARY: "a better-matched disparity raises the ceiling" for a model iff mean4 dREG_FRAME <= -0.005 AND >= 3/4
     clips improved.  "For every model" iff it holds for origin AND deliv.
  P1 interpretation: if additionally |mean4 dBLK_LOCAL| < 0.002, the gain is geometric alignment (the output sits where
     the real camera saw things), not appearance; reported as such, not as "less blur".
  Reported: dUNREG for RF-R1 and RO-R1 (the convergence part), dREG_CLIP, dBLK_LOCAL, edgeHF/GT, sharp, selfEdgeHF,
     hole fraction; the deliverable-minus-origin gap on R1 vs on RF; s*, o* per clip and the fit PSNR curve.
  Caveats stated with every RF number: ORACLE (fitted on the scored clip's own GT; per-clip DepthCrafter normalisation
     means a per-device constant cannot reproduce it; a deployable version needs metric depth + the camera baseline),
     and a PRODUCT decision (stereo strength x s*).

PROBE 2 (splat quality).  Step A (model-free, CPU): input decomposition of every V in {NA,B1,B4,ZB,SS4,SS4C} vs R1.
  P2A: V "lowers the input stripe energy" iff its INPUT stripeE/GT is >= 10 % below R1's on >= 3/4 clips AND its
     INPUT edgeHF/GT is never more than 10 % below R1's (no blur-for-stripes trade).
  Step B (renders, both models, 4 clips): every V passing P2A; if none passes, the single V with the largest mean
     input stripeE reduction (so "does either model's output move" is still answered).
  P2B: V improves a model iff mean4 dREG_FRAME (V - R1) <= -0.003 AND >= 3/4 clips improved AND the output's
     stripeE/GT falls on average.  dUNREG reported alongside.

SUCCESS CRITERION OF THE ROUND (for context, not this lane's gate): registered-GT LPIPS >= 0.005 better than origin
  and better on >= 9/12 clips, without blur or fake texture.  This lane can only show a 4-clip, oracle signal.
