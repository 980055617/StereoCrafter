SCALE_GT -- does GT-supervised diffusion-loss fine-tuning still degrade the model when it sees 100+ clips instead of 1-28?
lane deep_20261004/scale_gt, GPU 1, 2026-10-04 22:00 -> 2026-10-05.  Every number below cites the file it comes from (this dir
unless a path is given).  Pre-registration: PREREG.txt (22:45, before any training), PREREG_ADDENDUM_1.txt (23:30, data facts),

=====================================================================================================================================
0. ANSWER
=====================================================================================================================================
YES, IT STILL DEGRADES.  Trained on 754 registered windows from 215 different clips (vs 1-28 before), every evaluated checkpoint
(7 of 12: steps 250, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000, 2500, 3000) is worse than origin on the registered dev metric (+0.013 to +0.015
REG_FRAME LPIPS, 0/5 clips better at each) -> the pre-registered outcome "STILL DEGRADES".  The one-time test of the dev-selected checkpoint (step 250) agrees:
8 steps REG_FRAME 0.2430 -> 0.2896 (+0.0466, 95 % CI [+0.029,+0.068], 12/12 clips worse, one-sided sign p = 0.5^12 = 2.4e-4;
UNREG +0.0378, 12/12 worse; sharpness x0.856),
and it is not only 8-step fragility: at 25 steps it is also worse than origin at 25 steps (+0.0207 REG_FRAME, 12/12 worse;
UNREG +0.0156, 11/12 worse).
Scale is not irrelevant: at matched steps the 8-clip run is clearly worse (registered dev delta +0.016/+0.021/+0.017 at 250/500/1000
vs +0.013/+0.013/+0.014 for 215 clips, i.e. scale removes 20-35 % of the damage) and the weights move ~20-25 % less per step -- but
the sign does not change, the damage appears within 250 steps and then plateaus, so neither more data nor more steps is the lever.
Separately, the held-out denoising loss on unseen dev clips IMPROVES (-3.4 to -4.0 %) -- for the 8-clip run as much as for the
215-clip run (0.3007 at step 500 for both), so this shows the denoiser generalises in MSE, not a scale effect.  The MSE gain does
not reach what the sampler draws (8 or 25 steps): outputs get blurrier/hazier (sharpness -7 to -11 %), registered right-eye PSNR
rises (+0.16 to +0.22 dB) while LPIPS gets worse -- the posterior-mean signature -- and the model learns the real right camera's
colour response (+2,+2,+4 /255, an inter-ocular colour mismatch).  Removing that colour shift leaves the LPIPS damage unchanged,
so colour is not the cause.
SCOPE OF "CLOSED": closed is exactly this -- data scale alone (215 clips, 754 windows) does not rescue per-sample diffusion-loss
fine-tuning of origin's 15 up_blocks.3 attn1 tensors toward the globally registered real right eye, at 8 or at 25 steps.
NOT tested here (all differ from how the original model was trained): a larger trainable scope than 15 tensors in one block;
a continuous (log-normal) sigma distribution instead of the 8 deployment sigmas (1/8 of steps sit at sigma 0.002 where the loss
is ~1.0 and irreducible); batch > 1; and above all a target that is geometrically consistent with the condition -- after the best
global shift only 45.7 % of informative cells are within +-2 px, because the real baseline differs from the rendered disparity by
a depth-dependent factor (input_side lane, scripts/distill/runs/deep_20261004/input_side/TABLE_FIT.txt: oracle-fitted
disparity scale 0.35-3.45 on its 4 clips).  That mismatch is the leading untested
explanation of why this loss on this target produces haze.
