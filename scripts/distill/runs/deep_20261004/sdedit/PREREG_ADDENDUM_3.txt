WHAT HAPPENED
  0225 is the zero-hole clip (make_input_sbs_v1_ext8.log: "hole px 0").  Its origin sd1fill render's sdedit_log.json shows
  hole_px_window 0 and changed_px_window 0 (the source frames handed to the second VAE encode are bit-identical to the
  conditioning frames: the fill map is keyed by the md5 of the unfilled window frame and nothing was filled), yet the
  second encode's latents differ from the conditioning encode's in ~40 % of the elements (per window: mean |sf*dz|
  0.0016-0.0020, max 0.31-0.96, i.e. ~0.15 % of sigma_start 1.17).  So two encodes of identical frames inside one process
  are NOT bit-identical on this GPU stack (the first encode is reproducible across processes -- K1 -- so the difference is
  state-dependent kernel selection / rounding in bf16, not randomness in the inputs).  A3 assumed bit-exactness:
  "sd1fill passes iff its writer md5 equals the same model's sd1 render" (deliv) / "init latents unchanged" (origin, which
  has no sd1 render at S2).  Both checks therefore fail on 0225 for a reason that says nothing about the fill.
  The same encode-noise floor is present in every sd1fill render (it is part of what the variant does: its init source is
  re-encoded); on clips with holes it is small next to the fill's own footprint (e.g. 0301 mean 0.053) and both are small
  next to sigma_start.
CORRECTED GATE A3' (replaces A3 for zero-hole clips; check_k2_v2.py; decided before any S2 score)
  A zero-hole sd1fill render passes K2/K4 iff (i) its fill log shows hole_px_window == 0 and changed_px_window == 0 and the
  render finished (every map lookup succeeded), and (ii) the per-window latent difference to the conditioning encode is at
  the encode-noise floor: mean |sf*dz| <= 0.01 (a sanity bound, ~5x the 0225 values; it does NOT separate noise from a
  small real fill -- 0204's 0.0048 with 0.03 % holes is below it -- which is why condition (i), no pixel to fill and no
  pixel changed, is the actual no-op proof).  The output difference between deliv
  sd1fill and deliv sd1 on 0225 is REPORTED (mean |diff| in 8-bit levels, CPU) as the practical no-op measure.
  Every other K2 condition of check_k2_v1.py is unchanged.
