                      crackfill_COPY.fill_rowlin applied to all hole runs, window + 16 px margin).  71-99.98 % of hole
                      pixels lie in runs <= 3 px (stripes-lane logs), the rest in 4-8 px runs; filling all of them is
                      the task's "fill crack pixels" with no black pixel left in the init source.  The conditioning
                      latents, the CLIP embedding and the mask channel are UNCHANGED (the fill reaches z_src only).
