ADDENDUM 7 (2026-10-05 01:07 JST) -- descriptive robustness check, no rule or verdict changed.  Written after seeing
the detail statistics of 0204, 0052, 0125 only (no 12-clip aggregate, no LPIPS/NR beyond 0204).
  Issue: edgeHF evaluates every row at the REFERENCE's own top-decile gradient pixels, which partly are the reference's
  noise/compression peaks, so every row's ratio is pulled below 1 by selection (regression to the mean).  In R1 this
  biases the VAE-on-input stage (reference BR is one of the two images) but not the UNet stage (neither ORIGIN nor
  VAE_BR is the reference), i.e. it can tilt the edgeHF verdict toward "VAE" -- most on soft/noisy 4400-px clips.
  The b1/b2 band RMS (all valid pixels, no selection) is not affected.
  Check (CPU, detail_selfedge_r2.py, every row of every clip, same composites/valid set as M3):
    (a) edgeHF_self = mean |Laplacian| at each image's OWN top-decile gradient pixels (no cross-selection);
    (b) edgeHF_s1  = mean |Laplacian| at the top decile of the gradient of the Gaussian(sigma=1)-smoothed reference
        (noise-suppressed edge set), GT chain and BR chain.
  Stage retentions are recomputed with (a) and (b).  If the stage named by edgeHF changes under BOTH (a) and (b), the
  R1 edgeHF reading is reported as selection-sensitive and the band-based (b1) reading carries the conclusion.
