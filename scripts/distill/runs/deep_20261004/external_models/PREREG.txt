CANDIDATE CHOSEN (search done 2026-10-04 21:55-22:05; see CANDIDATES.txt in this dir)
  M2SVid (Shvetsova et al., Google; arXiv 2505.16565), official code github.com/google-research/m2svid commit
  11b0133093d6abfcc6ff953890edf05457975318, FULL-ATTENTION weights
  https://storage.googleapis.com/gresearch/m2svid/m2svid_weights.pt  (md5 b8618e8d8995042a72038f45c356f2f6, 4.98 GB,
  contains UNet + OpenCLIP-H image tower + 3 VAE encoders + SVD video VAE).
  Why this one: SVD-based like StereoCrafter, takes EXACTLY our kind of input (left video + warped right + disocclusion
  mask), additionally conditions on the LEFT video (information origin lacks: origin's UNet sees only warp+noise, and
  its mask channel weights are 0), single feed-forward step, paper LPIPS 0.180 vs StereoCrafter 0.242 on Stereo4D,
  fits 24 GB.  StereoPilot / StereoWorld (Wan2.1-1.3B) take only the mono video and choose their own disparity, so their
  right eye cannot be scored against our real right eye on the same geometry -> not chosen (reported only).
