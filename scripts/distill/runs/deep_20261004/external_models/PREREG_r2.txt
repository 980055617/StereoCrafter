CANDIDATE (search record: CANDIDATES_r2.txt)
  M2SVid (Shvetsova et al., 3DV 2026; arXiv 2505.16565), code github.com/google-research/m2svid commit 11b0133 (Apache-2.0),
  FULL-ATTENTION weights https://storage.googleapis.com/gresearch/m2svid/m2svid_weights.pt, md5
  b8618e8d8995042a72038f45c356f2f6 (verified), /mnt/ssd_data/deep_20261004/external_models/ckpts/.
  SVD-based; inputs = left video + warped right + disocclusion mask (exactly our inputs) and it ADDITIONALLY conditions on
  the left video (VAE latent concat + per-frame CLIP) -- information origin's UNet does not get.  1 feed-forward step.
  Paper (Table 1, Stereo4D, 512x512, 16 frames @5 fps): LPIPS 0.180 vs StereoCrafter-25-step 0.242.
