FINAL ANSWER
  No open model found today gives a clearly sharper AND more correct right eye than what this project already has.
  M2SVid, the only runnable open model that accepts our exact warp + mask, edges out ORIGIN on average (REG_FRAME
  -0.0057, 9/12 -- the program criterion is met only at its margin; the lane's stricter rule is not), is crisper than
  origin on its good clips, but is NOT better than the deliverable (REG_FRAME +0.0075, 4/12; UNREG tie +0.0008) and has
  two failure modes a viewer will see: foreground erasure / ghosting on close large-disparity subjects (0052, 0251) and
  window seams every 16 frames (seam ratio 1.97 vs origin 1.33).  The paper's 0.180-vs-0.242 margin over StereoCrafter
  (each method with its OWN warper, 512x512, 16 frames @5 fps) does not transfer to this setting: here M2SVid is also
  worse than StereoCrafter at 25 steps (REG_FRAME 0.2373 vs s25 0.2278).  Confounds not separated: 576x1024 is beyond its
  512 training resolution, non-overlapping 16-frame windows, F2 chunked decode, our soft occlusion map.  "Sharper" is
  only visual on the good clips (0301 0259 0225 0170); sharp/gtSharp over 12 clips is 0.89 (M2SVid) vs 0.94 (origin)
  and NR (0204 only) shows no advantage.  Mechanism of the failures: the mask-threshold hypothesis is refuted (0052
  overlay; dev 0040 t95 null); left-view reliance at close range is UNVERIFIED.  Single seed (cond_aug noise; the smoke
  rerun differed by up to 121 levels per pixel).
