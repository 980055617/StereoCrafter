VERDICT
KEEP 5 SLOTS: up3 provides 63.0% of the speed win at 576x1024
  (marginal method at the deployed resolution; 60.3 % at 1024x1792 and 59.7 % at 1920x1024, and the
  independent per-slot attribution says 60.5 / 59.4 / 59.1 %.  The honest band: 59-63 %, i.e. every
  estimate at every resolution is a clear majority.  The 63.0 % figure carries about +-5 points because
  origin sits in its denominator and the origin row has the largest spread, +-0.0040 s; that is exactly
  why the two methods differ by 2.5 points at 576x1024 and by <1 point at both hi-res settings.)
  Option (B) would surrender almost exactly that:
    576x1024   UNet/step -5.41 %  ->  -2.00 %
    1024x1792  UNet/step -20.56 % ->  -8.16 %
    1920x1024  UNet/step -21.87 % ->  -8.81 %
  i.e. at the deployed resolution the published speed claim shrinks from -5.4 % to -2.0 %, and the
  two head-line hi-res claims (-20.5 / -21.7 %) become -8.2 / -8.8 %.
  CAVEAT found while cross-checking on the deployed path (section 6): the first call into the first
  Mamba slot costs a one-time 5.07 s of kernel build+autotune per inference process, which nearly
  cancels the ~5.35 s of UNet time the 5-slot model saves on a 151-frame clip at 576x1024.  The
  deployed-resolution win is therefore real in UNet-MODULE time (which is what is published) but a wash
  end-to-end per process; at 1024x1792 / 1920x1024 the per-forward saving (0.77 / 0.89 s) swamps it.
  Quality does not enter the decision: the 2-slot Mamba alone is origin-equivalent on 12 clips
  (0.3931 vs 0.3933 lossless, section 7), as the 5-slot is.

2. METHODOLOGY, REPRODUCED EXACTLY
----------------------------------
Pass A, scripts/distill/bench2.py UNMODIFIED, driver scripts/distill/runs/slotbudget/bench_totals_v1.sh:
  synthetic UNet-only forward (no VAE, no sampler, no I/O); batch 2 (guidance 1.01 keeps the
  CFG-doubled batch); 14 frames; latent (H/8, W/8) + 1 mask channel; fp16; NO checkpoint loaded -
  the published bench timed the ARCHITECTURE via env only (MAMBA_SELF_ATTN_INCLUDE / _EXCLUDE,
  D_STATE=128, EXPAND=1, BIDIRECTIONAL_MODE=fwd, REPLACEMENT=gated_residual, INITIAL_GATE=1.0), so the
  protected deliverable checkpoint is never touched; 3 untimed warmup forwards, then 10 timed forwards,
  wall clock bracketed by torch.cuda.synchronize(), divided by 10 -> seconds per UNet forward = per
  sampler step (deployed = 8 steps); peak VRAM = torch.cuda.max_memory_allocated() after
  reset_peak_memory_stats() following warmup; exclusive GPU 0; one process per repeat; loop order
  reps-outer / configs-inner so thermal drift cannot alias onto a config.  Resolutions are written HxW.
Pass B, scripts/distill/runs/slotbudget/bench_slots_v2.py (copy of bench2.py's setup + timing protocol
  plus utils.module_timing hooks), driver bench_slots_v1.sh:
  phase 1 = the pass-A protocol with NO hooks (the quoted total), phase 2 = hooks installed on exactly
  the five level-0 spatial slots, 1 warmup forward, events cleared, 10 timed forwards.  Module time is
  INCLUSIVE of the slot's children: for a replaced slot that is the whole GatedResidualMambaSelfAttention
  adapter, which is the apples-to-apples unit ("time of whatever occupies the attn1 slot").  Hook cost
  is +0.002 s (+0.2 %) at 576x1024 and within the spread at both hi-res settings.
Configurations (all with gate 1.0, d_state 128, expand 1, fwd-only, gated_residual):
  origin  MAMBA_SELF_ATTN_INCLUDE=__nomatch__                        (0 slots replaced)
  mamba5  MAMBA_SELF_ATTN_INCLUDE='down_blocks.0.*,up_blocks.3.*'    (the shipped deliverable, 5 slots)
  mamba2  MAMBA_SELF_ATTN_INCLUDE='down_blocks.0.*'                  (2 slots, option (B)'s model)
Per-run verification, from bench2.py's own counters (per UNet forward) and the adapter log:
  origin: mamba_core=0  plain_attn1=16  origin_attn=0  gates=[]
  mamba5: mamba_core=5  plain_attn1=11  origin_attn=0  gates=[1.0]   total_replaced=5
  mamba2: mamba_core=2  plain_attn1=14  origin_attn=0  gates=[1.0]   total_replaced=2, and the adapter
          log shows [filter-skip] on up_blocks.3.attentions.{0,1,2}.transformer_blocks.0.attn1, while
          pass B reports class=Attention for those three slots and GatedResidualMambaSelfAttention for
          the two down_blocks.0 slots.  origin_attn=0 everywhere -> the re-parented reference attention
          is never evaluated, so no double-execution is inflating any row.

8. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE OPEN DECISION
----------------------------------------
(B) is free (0 GPU-hours, already-trained tensors) and keeps the quality win, but it surrenders 60-63 %
of the published speed win at every resolution, and the deployed-resolution claim collapses from
-5.4 % to -2.0 % of UNet time.  (A) keeps all five slots and must re-distil the step-distillation
objective onto the Mamba parameters, 13-35 GPU-hours, with the Mamba-side oracle already showing the
headroom is there.  The profile says the three up_blocks.3 slots are not a rounding error: they are the
larger half of the entire speed story, and they get MORE important with resolution, which is the
direction the project's published hi-res claims point.
