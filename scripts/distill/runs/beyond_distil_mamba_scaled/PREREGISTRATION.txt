sharpness-vs-GT ratio is closer to 1.0."  Its purpose was to avoid shipping an over-sharpened
checkpoint when two rungs are genuinely tied on LPIPS.  On dev the ladder is FLAT -- its entire span

monotonically with training step the rule then degenerates to "always ship the earliest rung"
(step200, 1.5 epochs, sh/GT 1.310 vs step800's 1.316 -- a 0.5 % difference).  A safeguard that fires
when its own precondition (a meaningful sharpness difference) is absent is mis-specified, so it is not
applied.  Both numbers are recorded here so a reader can check: the rule as literally written would
have selected step200 (dev 0.5472); the primary criterion selects step800 (dev 0.5464).
