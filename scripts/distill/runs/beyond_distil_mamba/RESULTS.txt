2. DECISION 2 -- SHOULD THE TWO down_blocks.0 SLOTS ALSO BE TRAINED?  NO.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Not a quality judgement, a hard constraint.  With trainable parameters in
up_blocks.3 the backward stops there and the whole down/mid stack never enters
the autograd graph; that is why both the origin-side run and this one peak at
~15.25 GiB.  Put trainable parameters in down_blocks.0 and the backward has to
traverse the ENTIRE UNet, and the 14-frame 576x1024 window then does not fit in
24 GB: the all-5-slot step-0 backward OOMs (see verify.json slot_grad_all5_error
and, in a clean process, verify_0301_all5.log / chain3.log).

A gradient-norm-per-slot comparison was deliberately NOT used to make this call:
AdamW is scale-invariant per parameter, so it normalises away exactly the
quantity such a comparison would rank, and the norms are confounded by parameter
count.  The measured constraint is the decision.

shape cost.  parse_profile_v2.py drops the first call of every module and prints
the raw column alongside, so the warmup is visible rather than quietly removed.
