DESIGN  The scheduler's step() draws one CUDA randn per step even at gamma=0 (unused), so a 6- or 5-step schedule
  would shift the RNG and give windows 1.. different initial noise than the 8-step reference.  PRIMARY renders
  therefore use SK_RNG_PAD_TO=8 (discarded draws after each window's last step) so every window starts from the
  8-step render's initial noise: a paired comparison that isolates the schedule.  This is a measurement device;
  a shipped T6/T5 sampler would run unpadded (same expected quality, different noise realisation).
