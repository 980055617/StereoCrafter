=== A SECOND CODEC EFFECT THE MP4V ERA WAS HIDING: a ~2/255 DC offset ===

Removing the codec made the scorer's leftPSNR on 0301 FALL from 46.77 dB to 40.25 dB, on a half
that is pass-through and now provably bit-identical to its source.  Cause, measured:

  one cv2-mp4v write + decord read, applied to the SAME pixel array:
      mean 0.29592 -> 0.28750   =  -2.149/255   (std gain 1.0017, PSNR(after,before) 37.06 dB)
  raw data, no model, same crop, splatting input vs train GT top-left quadrant:
      mean 0.29463 vs 0.28648   =  +2.079/255   (PSNR 38.92 dB)

The splatting inputs were themselves written by this cv2 mp4v writer, so their decord decode sits
~+2/255 above the train-GT reference; the output write applied a second -2.1/255, cancelling it to
within 0.07/255.  The mp4v era's "leftPSNR ~47 dB, constant per clip" was therefore reporting a
coincidental DC cancellation, not fidelity.  The true input-vs-GT level gap is ~39 dB.

Consequences, and how this lane handles them:
  * mp4v absolute LPIPS got a free DC correction that lossless output does not get, so
    lossless-vs-mp4v ABSOLUTE LPIPS is not a like-for-like comparison.  Only within-codec
    config-vs-config deltas are quoted as results here.
  * the offset comes from the INPUT, not the sampler, so it is the same for origin / s25 / g125.
    score_dcmatch.py re-scores with the variant's global mean matched to the GT's as a robustness
    check on every delta (see DCMATCH section in TABLES.txt).
  * under lossless writing leftPSNR is now IDENTICAL across configs of a clip (the left half is
    bit-identical), which is the direct evidence that the shared-bit-budget confound is gone.
