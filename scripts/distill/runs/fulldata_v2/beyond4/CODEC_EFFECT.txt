codec effect on measured LPIPS: min -0.0094  max +0.0086  |max| 0.0094
  -> CLIP-DEPENDENT IN SIGN and up to 0.0094 in magnitude, i.e. the same order as the whole
     12-clip s25 gain (-0.0138).  Any beyond-origin headline measured through mp4v carried
     a per-clip error of this size.

codec effect on the sharpness statistic: mp4v INFLATES sharp by +4.6% (0052) and +6.5% (0147),
  and is neutral on 0204 (-4.6%, the other way) and 0301 (0.0%).  It is NOT uniformly neutral:
  the two 4400x4400 clips are the ones mp4v flatters.  The over/under-sharp regime does not
  flip on any clip -- 0052 still overshoots GT (1.31x lossless vs 1.37x mp4v), 0147 still
  overshoots (1.59x vs 1.69x) -- but the reported margin was overstated.

leftPSNR: fell ~47 dB -> ~40 dB on every clip.  That is the DC-offset artefact documented in
  FAITHFULNESS.txt, not a fidelity loss; the lossless left half is bit-identical to its source.

DC-match robustness (score_dcmatch.py, origin_ll): LPIPS raw -> DC-matched
  0052: 0.4467 -> 0.4471  (shift -3.897/255)   |change| = 0.0004
  0147: 0.5269 -> 0.5267  (shift -3.951/255)   |change| = 0.0002
  0204: 0.2053 -> 0.2053  (shift +3.277/255)   |change| = 0.0000
  0301: 0.4351 -> 0.4345  (shift -2.419/255)   |change| = 0.0006
  -> max 0.0006.  The DC offset is essentially free in LPIPS; it does not carry the result.
     (score_dcmatch clamps to [0,1] after the shift, so a clip with large true-black areas
      gets a marginally pessimistic DC-matched number.)
