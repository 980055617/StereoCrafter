The 4-clip mean optimum IS near g1.25 (-0.0105), confirming beyond2's 1.25-1.40 estimate and
refining it downward.  But the PER-CLIP optimum has no common value:
  0147 : monotonically WORSE with guidance (+0.0026 / +0.0035 / +0.0036) -> wants g1.01, i.e. none
  0301 : optimum ~1.2 (-0.0283) and COLLAPSES by 1.40 (-0.0072, losing 3/4 of the gain)
  0204 : FLAT between 1.25 and 1.40 (-0.0111 vs -0.0117; the 0.0006 gap is at the measurement floor)
  0052 : FLAT-to-slightly-better at 1.40 (-0.0060 vs -0.0077)
So one clip wants guidance OFF, one peaks at ~1.2 and is on a cliff, two are flat out to 1.4.
The argument rests on the two large margins, not on the flat ones: 0147 is monotonically worse
(+0.0026 / +0.0035 / +0.0036) and 0301 collapses -0.0283 -> -0.0072.  Both are an order of
magnitude above the ~0.0006 floor implied by the DC-match robustness pass.
There is no single value that is right for this clip set -- which is the structural reason
guidance cannot be a global deployment default, independent of the size of the mean.

g115 is the SAFER point: 90% of g125's 4-clip mean gain (-0.0094 vs -0.0105) with a smaller
worst case (+0.0026 vs +0.0035) and less sharpening (4-clip mean sharp 0.0138 vs 0.0148).
