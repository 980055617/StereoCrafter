--- IMPORTANT CAVEAT ON THE 'OVERSHOOTS GT' COLUMN ---
That column uses the frame-wide `sharp` statistic (mean |horizontal difference| over the whole
frame).  VISUAL_READ.txt + ringing_metrics.py show this statistic is inflated by SPLATTING-STRIPE
ENERGY IN FLAT REGIONS, not by detail: for the deployed origin, stripeE/GT is 2.85x on 0052 and
4.83x on 0147, while edgeHF/GT (detail at the GT's real edges) is only 0.53 and 0.52.
In every texture-rich crop inspected, on every clip and frame, the GT is SHARPER than both origin
and g125.  So "7/12 overshoot GT" means "7 of 12 clips carry more total gradient energy than the
GT, most of it stripe artefact", NOT "7 of 12 clips look over-sharpened".
Read it as an artefact-amplification warning, which is what g125 does: stripeE/GT rises
2.854->3.173 (0052), 4.828->5.012 (0147), 0.975->1.295 (0301).
