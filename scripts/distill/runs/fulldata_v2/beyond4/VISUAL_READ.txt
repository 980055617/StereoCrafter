WHAT I SEE

0301 (origin under-sharp, the biggest g125 win).  GT: fine twigs, grass blades and bark all
separated.  Origin: a waxy smear -- twigs merge into blobs, the reflection in the water is mush.
g125: visibly more structure, individual branches and grass blades separate again, distinctly
closer to GT.  This is GENUINE DETAIL, not ringing: no bright/dark halo rims on the trunks, no
stair-stepping on the branch edges.  Clear visual win.

0052 (frame-wide 'sharp' says origin ALREADY sharper than GT).  GT: bicycle frames with visible
film grain, crisp spokes and brake cables.  Origin: smooth and waxy, the thin cables partly
dissolve.  g125: slightly crisper -- cable and spoke edges tighter, the '501' sign lettering a
little better defined.  No halos on the dark tube edges.  Marginal but real improvement.

0147 (frame-wide 'sharp' says origin ALREADY sharper than GT; g125 REGRESSES in LPIPS).  GT: fine
detail and grain on the white machine housing.  Origin: smooth, slightly washed out.  g125: a
little crisper AND noticeably higher local contrast -- the red band is more saturated, the dark
cables darker.  It reads 'crunchier' rather than more detailed.  This is the clip where LPIPS gets
worse (+0.0035), and the crop is consistent with that: contrast is being added where detail is not.

THE FRAME-WIDE 'sharp' STATISTIC MISLABELS 0052 AND 0147  (this is the important finding)

In every texture-rich crop, on every clip and every frame, GT is SHARPER than origin:
  clip  frame   GT      origin   g125
  0052   40   0.02970  0.02382  0.02657
  0052   75   0.02644  0.02209  0.02447
  0052  110   0.02934  0.02515  0.02807
  0147   40   0.01430  0.01195  0.01274
  0147   75   0.01493  0.01261  0.01318
  0147  110   0.01427  0.01167  0.01263

  0301   75   0.07996  0.03410  0.04495
  0301  110   0.06010  0.02753  0.03379
and edgeHF/GT (mean |Laplacian| inside the GT's top-decile gradient regions) is 0.53 / 0.52 / 0.27
for origin on 0052 / 0147 / 0301 -- i.e. origin is UNDER-detailed at real edges on ALL THREE clips,
including the two the frame-wide statistic calls 'already sharper than GT'.

The frame-wide number is inflated by SPLATTING-STRIPE ENERGY IN FLAT REGIONS, not by detail:
stripeE/GT (mean |horizontal difference| inside GT-flat regions) for origin is
  0052  2.85x GT      0147  4.83x GT      0301  0.98x GT
So on the two 4400x4400 clips, most of origin's 'extra sharpness' is disocclusion/stripe artefact.
'Origin already sharper than GT' should be read as 'origin already carries more stripe artefact
than the GT carries texture', NOT as 'origin is over-sharpened'.

WHAT g125 DOES TO THE ARTEFACT/DETAIL TRADE  (ringing_metrics.py, GT-defined regions, SCORE_STEP=8)

           edgeHF/GT (detail, higher=better)   stripeE/GT (artefact, lower=better)   haloFrac%
  clip     origin   g125    s25               origin   g125    s25                  origin  g125   s25
  0052      0.533   0.580  0.546               2.854   3.173  3.080                 39.1   41.5  39.7
  0147      0.517   0.544  0.536               4.828   5.012  5.599                 41.7   43.5  42.4
  0301      0.272   0.315  0.299               0.975   1.295  1.239                 24.9   28.9  27.8

g125 buys real edge detail (+9%, +5%, +16% of the GT's edge energy) but amplifies flat-region
stripe energy by +11%, +4%, +33% and raises the halo fraction by 2.4, 1.7 and 4.1 points.
That trade is COMPARABLE TO s25's on the same clips -- s25 lands at similar edgeHF with similar or
worse stripeE (0147: s25 stripeE 5.599 vs g125 5.012) -- but g125 costs 1.03x inference where s25
costs 2.5x.  Neither config introduces the ringing/halo signature that would disqualify it
outright; both amplify the pre-existing splatting stripes along with the detail.

ADDITIONAL OBSERVATION (0052 frame 40, crops/0052/0052_f40_3way_crop_300pct_nearest.png)

The crop lands on a poster with legible German text in the GT ("trinken ... furt").  Neither config
reproduces it: origin renders hallucinated glyph shapes that are simply wrong, and g125 renders the
SAME wrong glyphs with more contrast and crisper strokes.  So in regions the model has to invent
(disocclusions), higher guidance does not recover content -- it makes the invented content more
confident.  That is not visible as ringing, and LPIPS mildly rewards it (texture statistics), but it
is worth knowing that part of g125's "sharpness" is committing harder to a hallucination.
It is not a reason to reject g125 by itself: origin is equally wrong there, just softer.
